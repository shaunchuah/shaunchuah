# Shaun Chuah

Clinical Senior Research Fellow at the University of Glasgow and Honorary Consultant Gastroenterologist at NHS Greater Glasgow and Clyde. My work applies machine learning and generative AI to inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), and I build the software our translational research runs on.

## Highlights

- [Foundry120](https://www.foundry120.com/): governed research platform for translational studies, covering sample tracking, data orchestration and an agentic AI layer. More than 35,000 samples across five Scottish clinical studies.
- [ChatIBD](https://www.chatibd.com): guideline-grounded AI assistant for IBD clinicians, with sentence-level citations. Co-founded with Dr Beatriz Gros and Dr Nik Plevris.
- [Foundry120 Atlas](https://atlas.foundry120.com): public IBD datasets, scored for reuse.
